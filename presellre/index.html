STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The objections you were about to text us. Answered.

Is this MLM, dropshipping, or a guru course?

No. There is no downline, inventory, or affiliate army. Operation Atlas is a task desk for review, support, and operational queues. You enter as an operator.

Do I need experience?

You need to read what is on the screen, follow protocol, and be honest during qualification. If you can be trained, we train. If you fake experience, qualification cuts you.

How much time does it take?

The minimum cycle runs about 6 to 12 hours per week. Some people do more when work is available. We do not accept people who promise forty hours and disappear by Tuesday.

Do I need to quit my job?

No. The operation was built to fit after regular work hours, on a phone or laptop. If your employment contract prohibits outside work, read what you signed.

How much will I make?

The honest answer: it depends on the tasks you complete and what is available. Some first-cycle operators report a few hundred dollars. Some clear more when they catch a full queue. Some fail qualification and make zero.

Does it cost anything to apply?

The application is free. If accepted, any onboarding terms are shown clearly before a card is requested. No hidden charge at the end of a video. If the number does not make sense for you, leave. Simple.

Does it work on a phone?

Most of the queue does. Some desks require a larger screen. You hear that during qualification, not after you have already lost a night.

What if I am not a fit?

You get a no. No drama. No thirty-day retargeting circus. We prefer a clean no over a ghost operator halfway through a cycle.
